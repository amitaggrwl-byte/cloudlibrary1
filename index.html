Administration

Administrators use the Admin tab to answer feedback and manage circles. Feedback starts with Awaiting reply; choose Answered, Resolved or All feedback to find other items. Previous and Next load ten items per page. The text filter searches the current page; status and circle-category filters search the full list. Reply drafts stay in place during page changes in this session. Circle actions keep your filters and open sections. Refresh a list to see recent changes. Community health has a separate Refresh button. Maintenance can rebuild book search, rebuild reader and circle search, or recount totals. Each repair shows progress and its last completed run; normal edits update search automatically.

Scan an ISBN or find missing details
  1. ISBN is the first field in Add and Edit because it is the quickest way to identify a book. Scan the barcode or enter the 10/13-digit number, then choose Find details.
  2. Title, author, cover, year, genre and available series details fill empty fields. Catalogs do not always know the series or its book number; check these yourself.
  3. Your existing text and chosen cover photo are kept. Clear a field first to replace it from the catalog.
  4. Use the single Cover photo box to upload from your device or paste a secure image URL.
  5. For many books, scan, review and save each copy, or use the list and series helper for a whole set.
Find a book from its cover photo
  1. Take or choose a clear, straight photo of the front cover.
  2. Open Find details from a cover photo and select Read cover text. The optional reader downloads when first used and processes the photo on your device.
  3. Correct any misread words. Keep the title and author, removing slogans or award labels.
  4. Select Find matching books. These words go to Google Books or Open Library to look for up to five matches.
  5. Choose the correct book, check the filled fields, and save. Decorative lettering may not be recognised; ISBN lookup is usually more dependable.
Search by series and book number

Try Magic Tree House 10 or Magic Tree House 10th book. The requested number appears first when found; other matching books are labelled as alternatives. Small spelling mistakes are tolerated, but an unrelated title will not match. Load more results continues through further batches. If nothing appears, check the spelling, try fewer words, or clear the genre and availability filters. Saved edits update search automatically after the search index catches up; run Search again to refresh results.

About CloudLibrary

A small, real-life book-sharing community created by Amit Agarwal. Readers discover books, connect with people they know, and keep lending simple.

Add one book
  1. Enter a title and author.
  2. Optionally add series details, a rating, or a note.
  3. Use ISBN Scan when the barcode is clear, then check the result before saving.
  4. A cover photo is compressed on your device before upload. Camera photos up to 12 MB are accepted; the compact cover is usually about 300 KB.
Add a list or a series
  1. Build a review list, with one book per line: Book title | Author.
  2. For a series, use Series | # | Book title | Author, or enter its name, author, and number range in the Series helper.
  3. Add series to list only creates editable entries. Check or rename each placeholder title.
  4. Add reviewed books to my shelf is the only button that saves books. You can add up to 100 at once.
Borrowing a book

Only confirmed friends can lend to one another. Readers can hold up to three active books, keep up to five pending requests, and ask at most two friends for the same title at once. A loan is due after 14 days.

Returning a book

The owner may send a reminder. Once the physical book is back with the owner, the borrower selects Request return and the owner selects Confirm returned. The score uses the borrower's request time, so a delayed owner confirmation cannot create a late penalty.

Saved books

Use Save on a book in Search or a friend's shelf. Saved books remain on My Library and open Search again when selected.

Reader score and ledger
  • Everyone starts at 3.0.
  • Each listed book adds 0.15, up to 1.5 points.
  • An on-time return after 48 hours adds 0.5. Every fifth consecutive qualifying return adds another 0.5.
  • A late return loses 0.5; more than 30 days late loses 1.0; a lost book loses 2.0.
  • A return within the first 48 hours records a 0.0 ledger entry and does not build a streak.
  • Scores stay between 0 and 10. Friends, messages, reminders and declined requests do not affect them.
Circles

Circles are approved groups such as a school, grade, locality, club, genre, or fan group. Search for a circle name to discover readers who have joined it. Open Profile, choose Edit introduction, and use the small cross on a circle to remove it. Administrators create and archive circles.

Privacy and feedback

Email addresses are never shown. Profiles display only library-facing details. Send a bug, question, or idea below; administrator replies arrive in Inbox.
