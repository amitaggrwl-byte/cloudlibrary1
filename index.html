About CloudLibrary

A small, real-life book-sharing community created by Amit Agarwal. Readers discover books, connect with people they know, and keep lending simple.

Add one book
  1. Enter a title and author.
  2. Optionally add a series name and book number, cover photo, rating, or note.
  3. Use ISBN Scan when the barcode is clear, then check the details before saving.
Add many books or a series

For several books, use one line per book: Book title | Author. For a series, choose the first and last number, select Prepare list, replace the placeholder titles, then select Add listed books. Preparing a list does not add books by itself.

Borrowing a book

Only confirmed friends can lend to one another. Readers can hold up to three active books, keep up to five pending requests, and ask at most two friends for the same title at once. A loan is due after 14 days.

Returning a book

The owner may send a reminder. Once the physical book is back with the owner, the borrower selects Request return and the owner selects Confirm returned. This two-step handoff prevents an accidental close.

Saved books

Use Save on a book in Search or a friend's shelf. Saved books remain on My Library and open Search again when selected.

Reader score and ledger
  • Everyone starts at 3.0.
  • Each listed book adds 0.15, up to 1.5 points.
  • An on-time return after 48 hours adds 0.5; a late return loses 0.5; a lost book loses 2.0.
  • A return within the first 48 hours records a 0.0 ledger entry.
Circles

Circles are approved groups such as a school, grade, locality, club, genre, or fan group. Open Profile, choose Edit introduction, and use the small cross on a circle to remove it. Administrators create and archive circles.

Privacy and feedback

Email addresses are never shown. Profiles display only library-facing details. Send a bug, question, or idea below; administrator replies arrive in Inbox.
