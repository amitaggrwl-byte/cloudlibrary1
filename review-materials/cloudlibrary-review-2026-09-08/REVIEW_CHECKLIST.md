# Reviewer checklist

Please record the screenshot filename with every observation.

## First impression

- Is the purpose of CloudLibrary clear on the login screen?
- Does the interface feel trustworthy, friendly, and suitable for school-age readers?
- Are the logo, typography, colours, spacing, and icon style consistent?

## Navigation

- Can you tell which page is active?
- Are the signed-in reader, Help, Share, and Sign Out easy to find?
- On phone and landscape screens, are important destinations still visible?

## Adding books

- Would a first-time child understand the minimum information needed?
- Are ISBN, barcode scan, cover photo, and manual entry clearly distinguished?
- Is adding many books or a series understandable without experimentation?
- Are mandatory fields, optional details, and lending availability clear?

## Finding and saving books

- Are series results and requested book numbers easy to identify?
- Is the bookmark action understandable without adding visual noise?
- Is it clear whether a book belongs to a friend and can be borrowed?

## Borrowing and returning

- Is the difference between requesting, approval, active borrowing, requesting a
  return, and owner confirmation clear?
- Are pending, approved, denied, returned, lost, and reminder states distinguishable?
- Do actions look deliberate enough to avoid accidental use?

## Friends, profiles, and circles

- Does a profile provide enough context before sending or accepting a friend request?
- Are friend cards compact but still useful?
- Is opening a friend's shelf predictable?
- Is joining or leaving circles understandable?

## Inbox and history

- Can urgent actions be distinguished from completed information?
- Are filters and Load more controls obvious?
- Does history remain readable without overwhelming the current dashboard?

## Administration and Help

- Can an administrator quickly find health problems, unanswered feedback, circles,
  administrators, and maintenance tools?
- Is Help searchable, readable, and sufficiently step-by-step?
- Are failure cases such as unavailable borrowing, incomplete returns, and blocked
  deletion explained clearly?

## Responsive design

- Note any clipped text, horizontal overflow, occluded controls, or excessive scrolling.
- Check whether portrait and landscape layouts preserve the same capabilities.
- Identify controls that are too small or crowded for touch use.

## Suggested response format

For each issue, provide:

1. Screenshot filename.
2. What is confusing or visually weak.
3. Who is affected and how often.
4. Suggested improvement.
5. Priority: Critical, High, Medium, or Low.
